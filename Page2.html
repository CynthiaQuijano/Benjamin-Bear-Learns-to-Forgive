🚂 Chapter 2: The Broken Toy Train

One sunny afternoon, Benjamin invited Oliver Rabbit to play at his treehouse.

Benjamin brought out his favorite toy train. It was a special gift, and he loved it very much.

While they were playing, Oliver accidentally knocked the train down the stairs. CRASH! The toy train broke in half.

"Oh no!" cried Benjamin. "That was my favorite train!"

Oliver lowered his ears. "I'm so sorry, Benjamin. I didn't mean to break it."
