🍂 Chapter 3: Benjamin's Hurt Feelings

Benjamin felt very sad and angry about his broken toy train. He couldn't understand how such a happy day had gone so wrong.

"I don't want to play anymore!" Benjamin told Oliver before walking away.

Benjamin wandered through the quiet forest until he reached a beautiful autumn tree. He sat beneath its colorful leaves, thinking about what had happened.

As the leaves danced in the gentle breeze, Benjamin remembered how much fun he and Oliver always had together.

"I miss my friend," Benjamin whispered. "But I still feel so upset."

Benjamin looked up at the branches above him. He wished someone could help him understand what to do next.
