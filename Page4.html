🦉 Chapter 4: The Wise Owl's Advice

As Benjamin sat beneath the autumn tree, a gentle voice called down from above.

"Why are you so sad, little bear?" asked a wise owl sitting on a branch.

Benjamin looked up and explained what had happened to his favorite toy train.

"Oliver broke my train, and I feel so hurt," Benjamin said.

The wise owl smiled kindly. "Sometimes our friends make mistakes. Forgiveness does not mean we weren't hurt. It means choosing kindness instead of holding on to anger."

The owl reminded Benjamin of Ephesians 4:32, which teaches us to be kind, compassionate, and forgiving toward one another, just as God forgives us through Christ.

Benjamin thought carefully about the owl's words. Perhaps forgiving Oliver could help heal their friendship.
